Our Story

Serving the Treasure Valley Since 1965

Asqueta Glass has been the Treasure Valley's trusted glass company for over 60 years. Locally owned and family operated by the Lopo family, we continue the tradition of quality craftsmanship that our customers have relied on for decades.

Our team combines traditional craftsmanship with modern techniques, ensuring each installation meets the highest standards of quality and precision. From custom shower enclosures to commercial storefronts, we take pride in every piece of glass we touch.
